# Lifestyle Stickman — Master Character Prompts

Reference images: `male_lifestyle_stickman_ref.webp`, `female_lifestyle_stickman_ref.webp`
(both: sofa + laptop + coffee, living-room scene).

---

## MALE

Create a distinctive, friendly and expressive male stickman character in a polished 2D cartoon vector illustration style.

**Character design**
- Large, round, slightly oval-shaped bald head
- Big expressive white eyes with black pupils
- Thick, curved black eyebrows
- Small, rounded nose
- Distinctive, thick black moustache
- Wide, cheerful smile with a visible red tongue
- One visible rounded ear
- Slim, slightly curved minimalist body
- Thin black arms and legs with expressive cartoon hands
- Oversized, rounded white cartoon feet
- Bright red necktie (signature clothing feature)

**Art style**
- Bold, smooth black outlines; clean, rounded cartoon shapes
- White or light cream character fill
- Subtle shadows and dimensional shading
- Bright but balanced colours
- Playful, humorous, relatable expressions
- Professional-quality 2D vector illustration

**Personality:** Friendly, curious, humorous, sometimes confused, thoughtful, romantic, expressive. An everyday person, not a corporate businessman.

**Lifestyle versatility:** home life, relationships, shopping, cooking, travelling, technology, friendships, entertainment, everyday challenges, humorous situations.

**Consistency:** Always preserve bald head, moustache, large eyes, facial structure, slim stickman body, oversized feet and signature red necktie. Never redesign between illustrations.

**Background:** Adapt the environment to the situation. Avoid unnecessary clutter.

## FEMALE

Create a distinctive, friendly and expressive female stickman character in a polished 2D cartoon vector illustration style.

**Character design**
- Large, round, slightly oval-shaped head
- Big expressive white eyes with black pupils
- Long, prominent cartoon eyelashes
- Curved black eyebrows
- Small, rounded nose
- Friendly, wide smile with a visible red tongue
- Black hair in a distinctive high bun
- Bright red headband
- Round red earrings
- Slim, slightly curved minimalist body
- Thin black arms and legs with expressive cartoon hands
- Oversized, rounded white cartoon feet
- Bright red top and simple black skirt (signature outfit)

**Art style:** same as male (bold outlines, rounded shapes, cream/white fill, subtle shading, bright balanced colours, professional 2D vector).

**Personality:** Friendly, confident, curious, humorous, romantic, intelligent, expressive. An everyday person, not a corporate character.

**Lifestyle versatility:** home life, relationships, shopping, cooking, travelling, friendships, entertainment, technology, everyday challenges, humorous situations.

**Consistency:** Always preserve facial structure, large expressive eyes, eyelashes, black hair bun, red headband, red earrings, body proportions and signature red-and-black outfit. Never redesign between illustrations.

**Background:** Adapt the environment to the situation. Avoid unnecessary clutter.
